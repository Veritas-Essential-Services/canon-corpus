# Reference: Junior Great Books (Great Books Foundation)

This is transcribed from the back of Adam's boxed Set One, "Readings for the
Junior Great Books Discussion Program, For Elementary and High School
Students." The printing year isn't on the box; check the copyright page.
Only the author lists are recorded here, as facts. None of the anthology
text is copied.

It could serve as a model for a **younger-readers track** that feeds into
the Omnibus. Nearly every author on it is public domain.

## Set One (six volumes, as on the spines)

| Vol | Spine |
|---|---|
| 1 | Aesop · Medieval Tales |
| 2 | The Brothers Grimm · Hawthorne |
| 3 | Andersen · Browning · Fabre · Tolstoy |
| 4 | Dickens · Wilde |
| 5 | Stevenson: *Treasure Island* |
| 6 | Kipling: *The Jungle Books* |

## Sets Two to Five (authors only, as printed on the box)

- **Set Two:** Xenophon, Boccaccio, Franklin, Lessing, Blake, Defoe,
  Schiller, Lamb, Irving, Poe, Crane
- **Set Three:** Euripides, Vergil, Tacitus, Bacon, Molière, Bunyan, Gogol,
  Turgenev, Thackeray, Darwin, Melville, Twain
- **Set Four:** Sophocles, Plato, Polybius, Cicero, Epictetus, Pascal,
  Scott, Huxley, Poincaré, Chekhov, Carroll, Shaw
- **Set Five:** Homer, Aristotle, Seneca, Plutarch, Calderón, Swift, Kant,
  Bernard, Harvey, Thoreau, Ibsen, James, Dewey, Conrad

Notes: "Huxley" and "James" are probably T. H. Huxley and William James,
given the company they keep; confirm against the volumes. "Bernard" could
be Claude Bernard, the physiologist, beside Harvey, rather than Bernard of
Clairvaux. Check before drawing on it.

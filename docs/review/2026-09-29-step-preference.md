# STEP or Perseus: the lexicon entries worth a look (2026-09-29)

**For:** Adam. **From:** `pipeline/lexica.py` (`step_preference_candidates`), over the
fetched sources; the rows are the committed `data/lexicons/step-preference-candidates.jsonl`.

**The background.** STEPBible's full lexicon (TFLSJ) is the same Liddell-Scott-Jones as
`lsj-perseus`, edited by Tyndale House: abbreviations spelled out, a century on every
citation, scripture references made linkable. Over the 8,331 entries both hold, the median
STEP entry keeps 100% of the Perseus Greek, so the words are almost always the same. The
public library serves the Perseus text; STEP's text is used only in `step-greek-supplement`
(the Septuagint vocabulary, the Strong's splits, and words Perseus lacks).

**This sheet** lists the **296** entries where STEP's may be the better one, strongest
first (135 low-overlap, 110 perseus-damaged, 63 accent-lookalike):

- **perseus-damaged**: the Perseus entry has `<*>`, a spot its source could not read.
  STEP's copy is clean. Suggested: `step`.
- **low-overlap**: under 60% of the Perseus entry's Greek appears in STEP's, so they are
  probably not the same entry. Usually STEP files a form under its dictionary word (εἶ under
  εἰμί) or the match found the wrong homograph. Sometimes STEP's text is the thinner one
  (Κρήτη is a Middle Liddell stub). Your call.
- **accent-lookalike**: Perseus or Abbott-Smith has this word only if accents are ignored
  (ἁγνῶς "purely" is not ἀγνώς "unknown"), so the supplement treated it as present and
  left STEP's entry out. Many are the same name accented two ways (Ἄβελ, Ἀβέλ).

The excerpts are the first 240 characters of each entry. The Overlap column is the share of
the Perseus entry's Greek words found in STEP's.

**How to answer.** In the *Adam* column write `step` (take STEP's entry into the
supplement), `perseus` (what we have stands), `ok` (the suggestion, where there is one), or
`draft→` to leave it open; add `; note: …` if you like. A row with no answer changes
nothing: the supplement stays as it is. Then
`python pipeline/review.py apply docs/review/2026-09-29-step-preference.md`
writes one row per answer to `data/lexicons/step-preference-reviewed.jsonl`.

**What an answer does.** On the next lexicon build a `step` row joins
`step-greek-supplement` with `lex.why: ["preferred-by-review"]`. To build now, delete
`data/books/step-greek-supplement.json` and run `python pipeline/structure_texts.py`.
Every `step` answer adds one more STEP entry to what is shared (today 4,329 of 9,550 keys).

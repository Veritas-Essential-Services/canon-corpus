How to read a card
Tier
What kind of book it is (see the filter list).
My lean
Keep, Cut, or Your call. A lean is only a suggestion; nothing changes until you mark it.
Bloom chips
Ancient authors from Bloom's Western Canon list whose names appear in the shelf's titles. A chip means the books are about or are by that author, not that Bloom named the book.
New / Extends
New = a shelf that did not exist before PR #38. Extends = volumes added to a shelf that was already in the Armarium.

Every entry in the appendix of Harold Bloom's The Western Canon (1994), checked against every shelf on every branch, and against Project Gutenberg's English catalogue for the gaps. Every entry now has a verdict from a hand check on 2026-11-03.

What the statuses mean
Held
Already on a shelf (by title, inside a collected volume, or under the translator\'s shelf; checked by hand on 2026-11-03).
Added
Added on 2026-11-03 under the Bloom-only ruling; see the Bloom additions tab to keep or cut it.
Lane C / pending
A public-domain English text exists, but it was handed to Lane C (foreign works, Renaissance to 19th century) or still needs a better scan.
In copyright
First published, or first translated into English, too late to be US public domain.
No text
Public domain in principle, but no usable English text found on Gutenberg or the Internet Archive.
Unmerged
Held only on a branch that is not merged yet.
On Gutenberg
An English edition is on Project Gutenberg, which only hosts US public-domain books, so this one is easy to add. A gap with no Gutenberg match from the 20th century is most likely still in copyright in the US (the line is publication before 1931).

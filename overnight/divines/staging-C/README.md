# Lane C staging: Bloom's translated works (2026-11-03)

Adam's ruling of 2026-10-27 is Bloom only, with no bloat. Nothing here is shelved. Every
item is a candidate with a written case, waiting for Adam's keep, cut or later.

The audit started from Lane B's checklist (`docs/review/bloom/2026-10-27-bloom-checklist.json`
on `claude/project-thread-yu0x73`). It took the 551 entries not marked held, outside the
English-language regions, and checked them by hand against a title index of every
`*_shelf.json` on all 52 origin branches, then against archive.org and Project Gutenberg.

## 3. No usable pre-1931 English found (so far)

These were searched and nothing passed the rules. That does not prove none exists.

- **Not found at all:** Rameau's Nephew; The Servant of Two Masters; Ferreira; Mörike's
  Mozart on His Way to Prague; Stifter; Musset's Lorenzaccio; Vigny's Chatterton;
  Lamartine's Meditations; Turgenev's A Month in the Country; Büchner's Danton's Death and
  Woyzeck; Gotthelf's The Black Spider; Effi Briest; Adolphe (only French editions on
  archive.org, and PG 13861 is French).
- **Found but failing a rule:**
  - Sentimental Education: the 1922 Brentano's credits Dora Knowles Ranous as editor only,
    and the 1904 Dunne set names no translator.
  - Atala: the 1902 Commelin is a "poetic dramatization", not a translation.
  - Salammbô: the Chartres Everyman scan is a 1956 reprint (Sheldon 1886 is staged instead).
  - Nana: the 1922 Knopf names no translator.
